Hinder Rules Quick Reference

§6.1 — Right of Way

The player whose turn it is to play the ball is entitled to freedom from interference. The player who can legally contact the ball first has the right of way.

§6.2 — Avoiding Interference

Non-playing opponents must provide: unobstructed direct access to the ball after a reasonable follow-through, and freedom to hit with a reasonable swing. When a hitter is within arm's reach of the net, defenders may not enter the imaginary cylinder rising from the rim — doing so awards a point to the hitting team.

§6.4 — Player Choice

A player encountering a possible hinder may continue to play OR stop play. To seek a replay or point, the player must stop play immediately and say "Hinder."

§6.5 — No Replay or Point

No hinder outcome if: no interference existed; interference was minimal; player played on past the interference; player created the interference by taking an indirect route; or there was no opportunity for a return.

§6.6 — Point Awarded

Hindered team receives the point when: there was interference the opponent did not make every effort to avoid, AND the player would have had an opportunity for a return.

§6.7 — Replay Awarded

Point is replayed when: there was interference the opponent did make every effort to avoid, AND the player would have had an opportunity for a return. Any faults reset.
